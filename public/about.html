About Gaurai Hospital

Quality healthcare that is close to home, honest and affordable.

Our story

Gaurai Hospital started with a simple idea: every family deserves a trusted doctor and a reliable diagnostic lab in their own town.

Today we offer outpatient care, emergency services, in-house pathology and imaging, and a pharmacy, all with a team that treats patients like family.

Our mission

To deliver safe, ethical and affordable care with respect for every patient.

Our vision

To be the most trusted healthcare centre in the region.

Our values

Compassion, honesty, safety and continuous learning.

Our facilities

  • Emergency & ambulance
  • Pathology laboratory
  • Digital X-ray & ultrasound
  • Pharmacy
  • Operation theatre
  • Waiting lounge & parking
